This ad frames the choice for them: same craving, better outcome. The strikethrough does 90% of the persuasion work in half a second — perfect for a thumb-stopping feed unit.

Lean all the way into California.

Every other almond brand hides behind pastel bags and soft copy. Almondos already went the opposite direction — red, bold, bear-on-the-flag energy. The ads should double down on that, not soften it.

Buffalo is the volume flavor. It's the one that ends up on game-day platters, at tailgates, in the bowl at the party. That's the story this ad tells — position the bag where the wings usually go.

  • TargetSports interest, game-day snack buyers, 25-54, US-wide
  • PlacementFeed, Reels, connected TV cutdowns (15s)
  • Hook test"wings without the mess", "the tailgate almond", "buffalo, bagged"
  • KPIBundle attach rate > 30% (buffalo tends to sell with salt & vinegar)

What ships in the first 30 days.

Three concepts above become nine creative variants — three hooks per concept — plus 15-second Reels cutdowns of the top two performers. Full-funnel setup: cold prospecting on the winning hook, warm retargeting on bundle offers, catalog remarketing on cart abandoners.

The store is already dialed. The brand is already loud. The only thing missing is paid distribution that matches the ambition of the packaging.
